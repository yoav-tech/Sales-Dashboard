Cookie Policy

Last updated: July 6, 2026

1. What cookies are

Cookies are small text files stored on your device. We use them, and similar technologies such as local storage, to run the website and platform, remember preferences, and understand how the Service is used.

2. Cookies we use

  • Strictly necessary — authentication, security and load balancing. These cannot be switched off.
  • Preferences — remembering settings such as language and dismissed banners.
  • Analytics — aggregate usage measurement that helps us improve pages and features.
  • Advertising — measuring the performance of our own campaigns (for example Google Ads conversion tags). Set only where consent is required and given.

3. Your choices

You can control cookies through your browser settings and, where shown, through our consent banner. Blocking strictly necessary cookies may prevent parts of the Service from working. Where advertising cookies are used, we honour applicable consent requirements.
